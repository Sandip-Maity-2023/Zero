What it is & Why it exists

What is ZeroHunger?

A full-stack MERN web application that digitises the entire food-aid supply chain — from a donor's kitchen shelf to a community shelter's dining table. It provides a single platform where organisations post food requests, donors pledge supplies, and volunteers handle last-mile delivery, all tracked in real time.

Why was it built?

Every year, billions of kg of food is wasted while millions go hungry. The core problem is not shortage — it is coordination failure. There is no easy way for a restaurant with leftover food to know which shelter needs it, or for a volunteer to know where to pick up and drop off. ZeroHunger solves this coordination gap with a structured, role-based digital workflow.

Role-Based System — Who does what?

🏛️
Organisation
Community pantries, orphanages, shelters, and non-profits that represent communities in need of food aid.
  • Register with org name & registration number
  • Submit food-aid requests with headcount, date, location & details
  • View and manage all submitted requests
  • Track which donors and volunteers are fulfilling requests
Dashboard: /organization-home
🎁
Donor
Restaurants, caterers, grocery stores, event organisers, and individuals with surplus food to contribute.
  • Browse open food-aid requests from organisations
  • Pledge a food donation with size, type & pickup location
  • Choose delivery method (self-drop or volunteer delivery)
  • Track donation status in real time (pending → delivered)
  • View full history of past donations
Dashboard: /donor-home
🚴
Volunteer
Drivers, cyclists, and couriers who handle last-mile food delivery from donor locations to receiving organisations.
  • Browse available unassigned delivery jobs
  • Accept a job by providing NIC, vehicle number & telephone
  • Pick up food from donor's location
  • Deliver to the organisation and update status
  • View history of all personal delivery jobs
Dashboard: /volunteer-home
🛡️
Admin
Platform administrators who maintain trust, quality, and safety across all platform interactions.
  • Review and approve new user registrations
  • Monitor all pending approval requests
  • Manage and audit platform activity
  • Remove or suspend accounts if needed
Dashboard: /admin-home
